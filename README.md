# PhD-Training-School-in-Poznan

This repository contains materials from the **PhD Training School in Poznań**.

## Repository Contents

The repository includes:

- 📊 **Presentations** from the training school sessions and meetings
- 📓 **Jupyter Notebooks** containing exercises and practical assignments

The materials are intended to support the topics covered during the training school and provide resources for hands-on practice.

## Structure

The repository is organized into folders containing presentation materials and notebooks related to the individual sessions.

## Usage

Feel free to explore the presentations and use the notebooks to follow along with the exercises and assignments from the training school.
