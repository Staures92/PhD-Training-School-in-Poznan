### Presenting to the group

Keep it to 5 minutes: 1 minute source ID, 2 minutes on the three numbers, 2 minutes on the methodology
caveat. The goal isn't just to relay facts — it's to model the kind of critical reading habit that keeps
these statistics from being mis-cited later in your own work.
